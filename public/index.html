A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before your memory
becomes a keepsake.

Can I personalize my resin gift?

Yes. Share your preferred names, colours, photographs or flowers. We’ll confirm which details work best for your chosen design.

Can you preserve my wedding varmala?

Wedding flower preservation is part of our collection. Contact us as early as possible so we can discuss the flowers’ condition, handling and design before you send them.

How much will my creation cost?

Each project is personal. The final quote depends on size, design and materials. Send an enquiry for current prices and available options.

When will my order arrive?

Crafting and delivery times depend on your design and location. Please share your occasion date; we’ll confirm the timeline and delivery charges before you order.

How should I care for resin art?

Keep it away from direct sunlight, high heat and harsh chemicals. Clean gently with a soft cloth. Resin and preserved flowers can naturally change in colour over time.
